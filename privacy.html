Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Supreme Share for Android and Windows handles information. Supreme Share is developed by Supreme Share.

Supreme Share does not collect any personal or non-personal data. We do not run servers, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

How your files are transferred

Files are sent directly from your device to the device you choose. Bluetooth is used to find the other device and start the connection. The files then travel over Wi-Fi Direct, or over your own Wi-Fi network if you choose "Same Wi-Fi". The connection between the two devices is encrypted. Your files never pass through us, and we cannot see them.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information on your own device only. It is not sent to us, and it is not included in cloud backups.

Transfer history: device names, file names (up to 100 per transfer), number of files, size, date and speed. The Android app keeps the latest 500 transfers. You can clear it in Settings.

Trusted devices: the name and a device ID of devices you chose to trust, so the security code can be skipped. Trust ends after 90 days, and you can remove a device at any time.

Your settings, such as whether receiving starts automatically.

The Windows app also writes log files next to the app (SupremeShare.Discovery.log and SupremeShare.WindowsApp.log) to help fix connection problems. They contain device names and connection steps, stay on your PC, and can be deleted at any time.

Uninstalling the Android app removes all of this. On Windows, the trusted phones list is stored in %LOCALAPPDATA%\SupremeShare.

Information visible to nearby devices

While a device is ready to receive, Supreme Share sends a Bluetooth signal so your other devices can find it. This signal includes a short version of the device name and a connection code. Anyone nearby could see this name while the device is visible. On Android this happens only while the Receive screen is open, or briefly while connecting to a PC. The Windows app is visible while it is running.

Permissions

Nearby devices (Bluetooth) and nearby Wi-Fi devices are needed to find your devices and create the direct connection.

Location is required by Android for apps that use nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Supreme Share does not read, store or share your location.

Notifications are used to show transfer progress, and are optional. A foreground service keeps a transfer running when you leave the app or the screen turns off.

Third-party services

On Android, Supreme Share uses Google Play services only to show the system prompt for turning on Location. Its data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your operating system, device manufacturer or other apps on your device may collect data independently of Supreme Share. We have no control over their practices and recommend reading their privacy policies.

Feedback emails

If you choose "Send feedback" in the app, your email app opens with a message to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it. The message shows your app version and phone model, which you can remove. We use your email only to reply to you, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

Children

Supreme Share is not directed at children and does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if a feature is added that handles data differently. Any change will be posted on this page with a new date before it applies.
