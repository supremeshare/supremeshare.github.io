Supreme Share

From here to there. Without the cloud.

Send files directly between Android phones, and between your phone and your Windows PC. No cloud, no cables, no account.

Supreme Share for Windows

For Windows 10 and 11 with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Install the Android app on your phone too.

Download for Windows

This is a beta. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new and not yet code-signed: choose More info, then Run anyway. All versions are listed on the releases page.

Android

The Android app (Android 12 or newer) is coming to Google Play.

How it works

  • Bluetooth finds your nearby devices.
  • Files travel directly over Wi-Fi Direct, or over your own Wi-Fi.
  • The connection is encrypted, and a 6-digit code shows you are connecting to the right device.
