How it works

No pairing in Bluetooth settings and no shared account. The first time, both screens show the same code.

  1. Open Supreme Share on both

    On the receiving device, tap Receive. A PC running the Windows app is always ready.

  2. Choose files and a device

    Bluetooth finds the other device nearby. Check that both show the same 6-digit code.

  3. Files arrive directly

    They travel over Wi-Fi Direct, or over your own Wi-Fi, and are saved in Downloads.

Questions

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that are not code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. You can compare the file with the SHA-256 checksum on the releases page.

Will it work on my PC?

Your PC needs Windows 10 or 11 with Bluetooth and Wi-Fi turned on. Most laptops have both. Very old Wi-Fi cards can be slow with Wi-Fi Direct; if a transfer is slow, connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi and choose Same Wi-Fi.

Why does the Android app ask for Location?

Android requires Location to be on for apps that use nearby Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Supreme Share never reads, stores or shares your location.

Does it need the internet or a Wi-Fi network?

No. By default the devices connect to each other directly with Wi-Fi Direct. If both are on the same Wi-Fi, you can choose Same Wi-Fi instead.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and no account during the beta.

Something does not work. Who do I tell?

Email supremeshareapp@gmail.com, or use Send feedback in the app's Settings. Include your phone and PC model if you can.
